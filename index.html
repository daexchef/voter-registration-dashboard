Key data sources & caveats (August 2026)

API · scrape pipeline · grok_report.pdf seed

Eligible voters are best measured by the Voting-Eligible Population (VEP): U.S. citizens age 18+ who are not legally barred from voting. Registered voters appear on state rolls (active + inactive in most counts). North Dakota has no registration system.

  • VEP: 2024 Elect Project–aligned snapshot in data/states.json. electproject.org
  • Registered (live): IVP 2026 estimates scraped from World Population Review via POST /api/refresh and the 15-minute scheduler.
  • USAFacts: alternate national total from their registration article (usafacts.org).
  • API: GET /api/states · GET /api/status · POST /api/refresh · GET /api/geo/us-states
  • Operational truth: state SOS / elections offices — rolls change daily.
